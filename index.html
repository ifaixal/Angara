The problem

LPG, coal and wood harm health and air

LPG

Costly and polluting

Expensive for households. Releases CO2 and toxic CO.

Coal

Dirty and dangerous

Smoke, SO2 and NOx cause smog and lung illness. Mining is hazardous.

Wood

Smoke, lost trees

Indoor smoke harms lungs, and cutting wood clears forests.

Coal ash needs proper disposal

Ash left after burning coal must be disposed of safely. If it is not:

  • Toxic substances can leach into soil and groundwater.
  • Fine ash blows into the air and is breathed in, adding to respiratory illness.
  • Ash heaps take up land and degrade it.

Angarra is built on these problems: a low-cost fuel from agricultural waste, with ash that is reused, not dumped.

Our ash goes back into the soil as a conditioner.

Execution plan

Made by hand, with a drum and a small tin

The carbonising machine large companies use costs roughly Rs 400,000, without duties and delivery charges. We heat a sealed tin of husk inside a firewood-filled drum instead, so no machine is needed.

1Carbonise the husk

  1. Sun-dry the rice husk fully for one day.
  2. Fill the small tin tightly and press the husk down.
  3. Close the lid; make 3–4 nail-size holes in it.
  4. Place the tin inside the big drum.
  5. Fill the gap around the tin with firewood.
  6. Light the firewood so the tin heats from outside.
  7. Wait for white smoke, then continue until it turns thin and bluish.
  8. Cool fully (4–5 hours or overnight) without opening.
  9. Open the tin once completely cool.
  10. Take out the black, crispy husk charcoal.

2Shape the briquettes

The char is loose, so it needs a binder to hold together.

  1. Grind the char into powder.
  2. Add starch as binder.
  3. Give it shape.
  4. Let it dry. The product is ready.
